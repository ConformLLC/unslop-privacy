# Unslop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

## Short Version

- Unslop does not collect any data
- Nothing leaves your device
- No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads

## What Unslop Reads

- Posts in your LinkedIn feed, so it can score them and fold the ones that look AI-written
- Scoring happens inside your browser. Post text is never sent anywhere

## What Unslop Saves

- Saved only in your browser with `chrome.storage.local`. It is not synced and not uploaded
- Your settings: on or off, strictness, rule switches
- Your "Never hide" list of authors
- Counts of folded posts
- For recent folded posts: the post id, the author name, and the first 100 characters, so the popup can list them
- Recent post ids, so a post is never counted twice

## What Unslop Does Not Do

- No network requests of any kind
- No selling or sharing of data
- No remote code
- No clicking, liking, commenting, or any other action on LinkedIn for you

## Deleting Your Data

- Remove the extension from Chrome. That deletes everything it saved
